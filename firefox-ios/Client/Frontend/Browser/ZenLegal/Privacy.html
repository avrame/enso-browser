Ensō Privacy Notice

Last updated 21 September 2026

Ensō is a web browser built on Firefox for iOS. This notice describes what the browser does with your information. It is written to be read, not to cover us.

What Ensō collects about you

Nothing. Ensō has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. It does not count its users, measure which features they use, or report anything about your browsing to us or to anyone else. There is no account with us, and there are no servers of ours for your data to sit on.

What stays on your device

Your history, bookmarks, tabs, saved passwords, autofill details, cookies, site data, downloads and settings are stored on your device. Passwords and payment card details are kept in the iOS keychain. You can erase any of it from Settings, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Mozilla accounts and sync

If you choose to sign in, Ensō uses Mozilla's account and sync service — the same one Firefox uses. Your bookmarks, history, passwords and open tabs are then encrypted on your device and stored on Mozilla's servers so your other devices can read them. Mozilla holds that data under Mozilla's own privacy notice, not this one. Ensō is not affiliated with Mozilla. If you never sign in, none of this happens.

Zen spaces

Ensō can read and change the spaces you have in Zen Browser. That sync runs through the same Mozilla account as above: the spaces are stored encrypted in your own sync account, and Ensō reads them from there. Nothing about your spaces reaches us.

Extensions

Extensions you install can read and change the pages you visit, according to the permissions you granted when you added them. Those extensions are written by other people, and what they do with what they see is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. You can see what each extension was allowed to do, and remove it, in Settings.

Sites and services you use

Browsing the web means talking to the sites you visit, and they can collect information about you. Ensō blocks many known trackers by default, but it cannot stop a site from seeing the requests you make to it. Searches go to the search engine you have chosen, which has its own privacy policy.

Some features inherited from Firefox contact Mozilla's servers when they are enabled: search suggestions, tracker blocklists and search configuration. These carry the request needed to answer them, and are subject to Mozilla's privacy notice.

Children

Ensō is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone — including them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version ships with the app and the date above changes with it.
